Science has long sought to understand the world and how it behaves.
To this effect scientist have looked at the world around them and tried to recreate phenomena in controlled environments.
This methodology has used the limiting and asymptotic cases to simplify expressions and in doing so found approximate solutions.
These simplifying assumptions can give explicit solution forms that only in specific circumstances capture the complexity of the studied problem.
This limitation has, in part, been due to simple equipment for monitoring the experiment.
Emerging technology can minimize this factor and bridge the gap from theoretical and experimental data.
