\subsection{Integrating Factor}

For the general PDE, $u_t = \mathcal{L}u + \mathcal{N}\left(u,t\right)$, multiplication by the exponetial $ e^{-Lt} $ reduces the problem to:
\begin{align*}
    v_t &= e^{-Lt}\mathcal{N}\left(e^{Lt}v\right) \\
    v   &= e^{-Lt}u
\end{align*}

For purely linear problems this method gives an explicit solution
\begin{equation}
    u\left(x,t\right) &= u\left(x,0\right)e^{Lt},
\end{equation}

That can be effectivly computed using matrix products.
