\section{Decay of Fouirier coefficients}
Intent: Present an explanation for the limiting factors of the recovered symbols. 

The decay of the Fourier modes are dependant on the smoothness of the function. For a function in $C^k(\mathcal{S})$ we have that,
\begin{align}
    |\hat{f}(n)| &\le |\frac{1}{n^k}\cdot \int_\mathcal{S} f^{(k)}(x) e^{-inx} dx| \\
    &\le \frac{C}{|n|^k}
\end{align}

For functions in the Schwartz space this definition can be expanded such that
\begin{align}
    |\hat{f}(n)| &\le \frac{C}{(1+|n|)^k} \\
    &\le C e^{-|n|\cdot k}
\end{align}

This last result is particularly interesting for us, as this gives exponential decay in the modes, and as such
one is limited by the accuracy of the machine for values of $n$, lets use $k=2$ and we find that at a given accuracy of $10^{-14}$ 
gives $n = 16$
